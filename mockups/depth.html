Depth pass mock-ups

Frames for slices S2–S6 of the forge depth pass (docs/specs/forge-depth.md), drawn at Morgan's Pixel width (443 CSS px). The close views are real screenshots of the game with the new parts drawn over them.

Too hard −2
Hold longer to strike harder. Hot metal wants a light touch.

2a. Strike force: the ring fills while the finger is down (here about 60%) and the strike lands on lift. A too-hard strike pops "Too hard" with its points. The force hint shows in the close panel the first 3 times the anvil is reached.

Slag first −1
Knock out the dark slag first. The spots beside it won't shape until it's gone.

2b. Slag: 3 dark pits between targets (the default knife mix: round(4 × 0.5 + 1 × 0.8) = 3). A strike on a spot held by slag pops "Slag first". A strike on a pit clears it with a puff and pops "Slag out".
